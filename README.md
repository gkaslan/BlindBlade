# BlindBlade
Our game is a samurai-themed title. At the start, we’ve included options for the story, a "how to play" guide, settings, and the game itself. The story section covers the game's narrative, while the settings menu allows you to adjust sound and music. The "how to play" section explains the controls, and the "play" option lets you jump into the game. You control the samurai using the WASD keys. Since our samurai wears a blindfold, he perceives his surroundings through the sound waves generated by clashing swords—this is how we integrated the "bounce back" theme. Enemies spawn at random locations and head straight toward you to attack. You can block these attacks with a right-click and then strike back with a left-click. As you defeat enemies, your level increases, and the challenges become progressively harder. We’ve included a boss fight at level 6, preceded by a cutscene. The boss shares the same core mechanics as the regular enemies but possesses 8 health points. Once you defeat the boss, the game concludes.
